Orlando to Tampa airport shuttle

Ride from Orlando International Airport to Tampa International Airport on a shared airport-to-airport shuttle.

Traveling the other way? Tampa to Orlando

TPA Shuttles runs a shared airport-to-airport shuttle from Orlando International Airport (MCO) to Tampa International Airport (TPA). The trip is about 92 miles by road, typically about 1 hour 45 minutes of driving, depending on traffic. At Orlando, you meet the shuttle at Terminal B, Level 2, Door B25. Book online in four steps or call 813-523-7346 — we confirm your departure time and fare before you pay.

Before you ride

  1. Book your seat

    Book online or call us with your travel date, flight details and number of passengers. We confirm your departure time and fare.

  2. Collect your bags

    Flying into Orlando? Collect your checked bags first, and call us if your flight is delayed. Not flying in? Be at the meeting point before your departure.

  3. Go to the meeting point

    Follow the steps on this page to the MCO meeting point and look for your shuttle.

Where to meet the shuttle at Orlando (MCO)

Terminal B, Level 2, Door B25

  1. Collect your bags at baggage claim.
  2. Make your way to Terminal B, Level 2 (arrivals).
  3. Exit at Door B25.
  4. Wait outside Door B25 for your shuttle.

We confirm these details with you when you book.

Arriving at Tampa International Airport

The shuttle takes you to Tampa International Airport (TPA). We confirm the exact drop-off point when you book. If you’re catching a flight, allow time for check-in and security: the drive typically takes about 1 hour 45 minutes. Interstate 4 traffic can add time, especially on weekday afternoons and holiday weekends.

This is an airport-to-airport ride. It doesn’t continue to hotels, theme parks or the cruise port; ask about a private ride if you need that.

Luggage and passengers

  • Luggage

    One standard checked bag (up to 50 lb) and one personal item per adult passenger; additional bags may carry a fee.

  • Children

    Car seats are not provided. Young children must ride in an age-appropriate child seat supplied by the passenger.

    Call us before you travel to talk through mobility devices or boarding assistance.

Questions about MCO to TPA

How long does the ride take between Tampa and Orlando airports?

The airports are about 92 miles apart by road. The drive typically takes about 1 hour 45 minutes. Interstate 4 traffic can add time, especially on weekday afternoons and holiday weekends. Leave plenty of time before a connecting flight.

What are the departure times and fares?

We confirm your departure time and fare when you book. You don’t pay anything until you’ve agreed to the fare.

Does the shuttle stop anywhere between the airports?

No. This is an airport-to-airport service — it doesn’t include hotel, theme-park, cruise-port or home pickups or drop-offs. For other destinations, ask about a private ride.

Where do I meet the shuttle?

See the meeting-point guides for Tampa International Airport and Orlando International Airport. We confirm the meeting point when you book.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Call us as soon as you know your flight is delayed, on 813-523-7346. We’ll tell you what options are available for your ride.

How much luggage can I bring?

One standard checked bag (up to 50 lb) and one personal item per adult passenger; additional bags may carry a fee. We confirm these details with you when you book. Tell us about strollers, sports equipment or other large items in advance.
