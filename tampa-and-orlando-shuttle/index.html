Tampa to Orlando

Board at Tampa International Airport and ride to Orlando International Airport.

Where to meet the shuttle at Tampa

Blue Express, Door 104 (downstairs)

  1. Collect your bags at baggage claim.
  2. Find the Blue Express sign on the third floor.
  3. Take the elevator or escalator downstairs to Door 104.
  4. Wait outside Door 104 for your shuttle.

Orlando to Tampa

Board at Orlando International Airport and ride to Tampa International Airport.

Where to meet the shuttle at Orlando

Terminal B, Level 2, Door B25

  1. Collect your bags at baggage claim.
  2. Make your way to Terminal B, Level 2 (arrivals).
  3. Exit at Door B25.
  4. Wait outside Door B25 for your shuttle.

Questions about this route

How long does the ride take?

The drive between Tampa and Orlando airports typically takes about 1 hour 45 minutes. Traffic and weather can make it longer, so leave plenty of time before a connecting flight.

What are the departure times and fares?

Contact us to confirm current departure times and fares. Fares depend on the day and the size of your group, and we’ll confirm yours before you travel.

Does the shuttle stop anywhere between the airports?

This is an airport-to-airport service. It does not include hotel, theme-park, cruise-port or home pickups or drop-offs. For other destinations, ask about a private ride.

Where do I meet the shuttle?

See the meeting-point guides for Tampa International Airport and Orlando International Airport. We’ll confirm the meeting point when you arrange your ride.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Call us as soon as you know your flight is delayed, on 813-523-7346. We’ll tell you what options are available for your ride.

How much luggage can I bring?

One standard checked bag (up to 50 lb) and one personal item per adult passenger; additional bags may carry a fee. Please confirm the details when you arrange your ride. Tell us about strollers, sports equipment or other large items in advance.
